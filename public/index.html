      Delegated work moves through a pipeline. These three lanes are the points where it needs you.

      1. Agents Work you handed to an agent that is in flight — queued, in progress, or blocked. The shared agent backlog.
      2. Human An agent paused to ask you a question and can't continue until you answer. Drag to hand-order what to answer first.
      3. Review An agent finished and handed the work back for your approval. Approve to accept, or reopen to send it back.

      delegate → Agents → Human (answer) or Review (approve)

      Your own to-dos live under Tasks — work you never delegated at all.
